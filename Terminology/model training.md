# model training

## Definition
> Model training is the process of “teaching” a [[machine learning]] model to optimize performance on a training dataset of sample tasks relevant to the model’s eventual use cases. If training data closely resembles real-world problems that the model will be tasked with, learning its patterns and correlations will enable a trained model to make accurate predictions on _new_ data.
