# neural networks

## Definition

Collection of networks chained together to perform specific tasks. 

[Source](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/neural-networks#741977106)

> A neural network is a machine learning model that stacks simple "neurons" in layers and learns pattern-recognizing weights and biases from data to map inputs to outputs. (Fangfang, n.d)


[Source](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-vs-machine-learning-vs-deep-learning-vs-neural-networks?mhsrc=ibmsearch_a&mhq=machine%20learning%20vs%20deep%20learning)

>Training data teach neural networks and help improve their accuracy over time. Once the learning algorithms are fined-tuned, they become powerful computer science and AI tools because they allow us to quickly classify and cluster data. Using neural networks, speech and image recognition tasks can happen in minutes instead of the hours they take when done manually. Google’s search algorithm is a well-known example of a neural network. (IBM, n.d)
