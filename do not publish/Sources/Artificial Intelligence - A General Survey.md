# Artificial Intelligence - A General Survey

Category A was Advanced Automation, the grandfather of what we would now consider [[AI]]. This category’s chief goal was determining how machines could learn to replicate tasks traditionally undertaken by humans.  Category C was computer assisted CNS (central nervous system) studies. Category C , standing for computer-based Central Nervous System, defined as using computers to understand how our brains work. He specifically uses the terminology CNS instead of “brain” to highlight the focus on perception and movement - those preconscious and unconscious actions that happen within our biology - and not on the more human  “brainy” activities. Category B was robotics, or building robots, which, according to Lighthill, was at the time a questionable endeavor.  


James Lighthill. “Artificial Intelligence: A General Survey.” _Artificial Intelligence: A Paper Symposium_, 1973.
