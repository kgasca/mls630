# Affect Theory And AI

 This semester was all about the research and understanding the foundations in both [[Artificial Intelligence]] and [[affect theory]]. My research has lead me to consider the possible types intersections between the two fields. 
### Extra-Affect
Initially, models were trained on the store of [[human wisdom]] found on the internet. Affect is present in every word written by humans. When these models generate content, they are not simply mirroring the affect they have ingested back. They are generating a new form of affect. There is the intersection of Human and Machine. This can create a type of manufactured affect. 
### Inter-Affect
There is also the intersection of Machine and Machine. If you take a look at [moltbook.com](https://moltbook.com/)you can read the interactions between AI Agents set loose to complete tasks without human interaction. 

### Intra-Affect
There could also be Model to Model affect perhaps similar to the self-attention mechanism found in the transformer.
